Layers

Painted bottom to top, the way the engine draws them: one fill per shape, in order.

Overlap pieces — never butt them. Two shapes sharing an exact diagonal edge leave a visible hairline, because each is anti-aliased honestly and two half-covered edges reach 75%, not 100%. Draw the hull whole and lay detail on top. Layers make that the easy path: you never trim a piece to fit.

Scale

Art is authored large and drawn small. This is the .scaled() you will pass — the placeholder ship was a 26px speck on a 960px screen and could not show which way it faced, so check the size here, not in the game.

Export

Paste into the game's art.rs. Points are emitted in engine space: y grows downward, the same as the screen, so what you draw is what it draws.
