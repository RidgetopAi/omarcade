Lean — where the squeal lives

The bar is cornering(), 0 to 1. The marks are the track's real bends, so you are tuning against corners you actually drive rather than against an abstract number. Squeal should be audible at GENTLE-to-FIRM — while the car is still holdable — so it warns you before you are in trouble.

Oscilloscope

Tyre squeal is STICK-SLIP: the tread grips, releases, and grips again many times a second. On the trace that is a fast carrier inside a slower ripple. Flat noise with no ripple is wind, not rubber.

Where it starts

THE DECISION YOU MADE. Squeal begins at THRESHOLD and is fully present by FULL. Setting threshold below the Firm mark is what makes the car talk to you before it lets go — a warning rather than a report.

The screech

An OSCILLATOR, not filtered noise — that was the "sounds like static" bug. A bandpass passes a BAND (114 Hz wide at Q=11) and the ear reads a band of noise as noise wherever you put it. A pitch needs energy in a line.
VIBRATO — the tyre catching and releasing. Without it a steady oscillator is a test tone; this is what makes it a tyre.
STICK-SLIP — the grip-release cycle, on the amplitude of both layers. Rate rises with speed.
GRIT — road noise UNDER the screech. This layer is allowed to be static, because it is not carrying the pitch.
