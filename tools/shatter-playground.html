Partial breaks

A reinforced brick's first hit throws this share of a full break. The plan wants damage and shatter to be one system, partial — that is what keeps it cheap.

Screen shake

Shake fires on an armoured break and on a lost ball — never on an ordinary break, or the whole game would wobble. Dragging either slider below shakes immediately whatever mode you are in, so the control is always live.

⚠ The plan caps this at ~4 units, decayed fast. Shake is the effect most likely to be too much, and it makes people motion-sick before they can say why. Judge it on the armoured break and on a lost ball, not on a plain one.

Theme

⚠ Chips are drawn in the brick's own colour — they are made of the brick, not a puff of generic dust. So the burst has to be checked under a real theme: S5 killed two colour choices that read fine in code.
