The start

The HUD counts remaining.ceil() — 3, 2, 1 — so a tick belongs on each CHANGE of that number, landing exactly when the digit changes. Ear and eye describing one countdown, the same rule the rumble strips follow.
Listen to the GAP before green. Three even ticks then a beat of nothing is what makes a start tense; a fourth tick on time is just counting.

Checkpoint

Banking time — relief, where the HUD flashes +seconds. Two notes RISING, because time going up should sound like going up.
LAP and FINISH are the same shape, moved. A lap is the checkpoint a fifth higher with a third note; the finish is longer and resolves.
