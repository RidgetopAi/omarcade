The grid and its palette are emitted together — they are the pair that has to stay in sync, and a grid carrying a letter the palette does not know is a Sprite::new panic. Load reads both back.

Saved palettes

The palette alone, without a sprite — so eight billboards can share one set of colours. Loading a palette keeps your current art; letters it does not define stay as they were.

Assets saved in this browser

Grid + palette together, under a name — click to load, × to delete. The name becomes the Rust const. Unsaved work is auto-restored when you reopen the page. art.rs stays the real archive; this shelf lives only in this browser.

Livery & custom
