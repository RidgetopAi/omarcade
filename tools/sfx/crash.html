The shape, against the fireball

BURN_TIME is 1.4 s — a real budget from crash.rs, not a guess. The sound has to be over when the fire is. The bars are the three stages; the line is the envelope they add up to.

Oscilloscope — the last crash

An impact starts at full deflection and decays. If the trace ramps UP at the start, the hit has no attack and will read as a whoomph rather than a collision.

Metal deforming. A few uneven hits rather than one — this is what stops it sounding like a single drum.
